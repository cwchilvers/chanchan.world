luster

Youthful illusions
Finally shatter
Kicking pieces
Watch them scatter

It's no use
No will left to muster
Every little shard
Has lost its luster

Pick up the pieces
Cut by the shard
Out pours the blood
Leaving one scarred

Fooled by pretense
And trod all over
Crows feast on the guts
Then flock to another

Friendship holds
A distance restrained
A line between
What's wanted, what's gained

Then comes love
The line disappears
Restraint no more
The taking appears

A gilded coat
Core made of tin
Shaped like a heart
Pulsing with sin

Young and naive
New to this place
Enabling pain
And calling it grace

Fall to my knees
Accepting loss
The devil approaches
Wearing a cross

Longed so hard
For a connection
Weighed the costs
And chosen protection

Safety found
In being alone
Call it reclusive
I call it home

I wish so hard
To see an end
Or pray to God
I never find love again
